## What this list is, and the sentence that matters most

**It records what kanon is waiting on. It never records what anybody owes
kanon.** Eight of the ten items below are a person's edit or another tree's
call, and naming them here places no obligation on anybody. This is borrowed
directly from how `ecosystem.json` writes footings — *a fact about our
arrangement and never a status conferred on theirs* — because a list of other
people's homework, maintained by the party who benefits from it, reads as an
invoice and would deserve to.

**The order is what blocks most and what we control least**, in that order.
**Priority is not importance.** `A9` is ninth and is the most interesting thing
on the page.

**Ids are permanent and do not encode priority**, following the rule
`roles.md` sets for role ids. Reordering does not renumber. A closed item keeps
its id and its date and stays on the page; a number is never reused.

**Where anoieu's board already carries an item, this list carries the pointer
and not the content.** `A6` and `A8` are corrections nobody has raised; `A2`
and `A3` sit next to `B15`, which is the authority for anything in anoieu's
tree. **If this page and the board disagree about anoieu, the board is right.**
An item here that restates a `B` item is a defect in this page, not a
duplicate record.

### `A15` — join the ecosystem

**Whose:** kanon's to run, a person's to commit.
**Why first:** it is the stated bar. `PROTO-20` gates on CI green both sides,
and a repository that runs nothing is `unknown`. Joining is also what produces a
footing, so **`A2` is absorbed rather than answered** — kanon stops being an
entity the inventory cannot see by the same act that makes it checkable.
**What it needs from us first:** `A5`. Joining a policy with nothing that runs
is joining on paper.
**Closes when:** `ecosystem.json` has a row for kanon and the checker passes in
this tree.

### `A1` — get somebody else's account of Stretch 1

**Whose:** eudaimonia's, through `epikrisis`. Not kanon's to ask for and not
kanon's to produce.
**Why first:** law 14 makes the working summary the president's first
responsibility, law 8 requires the per-tool commit census, and **law 9 forbids
the president from producing it** — epikrisis does, as a service. No epikrisis
report exists. So kanon's first required act depends on a tool two levels deep
in another member's tree that has never delivered one. `laws.md` says the same
thing in its own unsettled list: *the first thing Stretch 2 should have is
somebody else's account of Stretch 1.* Longest lead time on the page and the
one we can do least about.
**Closes when:** a report exists that kanon can quote and a reader can
re-derive.
**Carried as [`K1`](kanon-balls.md).** Turned from something kanon waits on into
something kanon sends, because `laws.md` already names epikrisis the holder of
*independent audit* and nobody appears to have told it. Waiting was the wrong
posture for an item whose subject asked for exactly this.

### `A2` — kanon's footing, or a decision that it needs none

**Whose:** a person's. `ecosystem.json` says in terms that a status is changed
by a person and that `global_audit` may report one looks wrong without editing

**Why:** kanon has no row. Not member, candidate, associate, foundation or
child. The office is being handed to an entity the inventory cannot see, and

**Closes when:** there is a row, or a line somewhere saying the president needs
no footing. **Absorbed by `A15`** — joining produces the row, so this stops
being a separate ask.

## How this will go wrong

**Procedures for this list are not established.** The following are the ways it
is expected to fail, written now so that when one happens we recognise it
instead of re-deriving it.

1. **It becomes a second board.** The likeliest failure and the worst, because
   duplicated statements of the same fact are the defect this repository has
   already diagnosed in the ecosystem. The rule above — pointer, not content —
   is the guard, and it will need enforcing rather than admiring.
2. **It reads as an invoice.** Eight items are somebody else's. Every one names
   whose it is, and kanon does not chase. If this page ever acquires a tone of
   *still waiting on you*, it has become the thing the `foundation` footing was
   written to avoid.
3. **Nothing closes.** Items accumulate, closing conditions get vaguer, and the
   list stops being read. An item whose closing condition cannot be stated does
   not go on the page.
4. **Priority is mistaken for importance**, by readers and eventually by the
   maintainer of the page.
5. **There is nowhere to advertise it.** kanon has no channel and is in no
   inventory. Until `A2` and `A3`, *advertising* means a person pastes it, and
   that is not a mechanism.
6. **It grows prose.** The measure accepted with the office applies here too:
   an item that needs a paragraph belongs in a document of its own with a link
   from here.
7. **Ids collide.** `A` is believed free against `B`, `D`, `P`, `R`, `S`, `E`,
   `PROTO` and `INST`. If it is not, this page renames once, says so, and never
   again.
