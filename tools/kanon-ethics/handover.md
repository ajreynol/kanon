# Ethics — handover

**Kept current, not written at the close.** This office records the conduct of
the presidency, so every entry names what it cost.

## Established

- **kanon accepted the office one message after arguing the governance layer had
  outgrown what it governs.** The objection is recorded against itself as
  objection 5, and the measure accepted with the office is that `docs/` must not
  grow relative to the code by the close of the term.
- **The same-owner loophole, both halves.** One owner is what makes this handoff
  safe and equally what stops it counting as a separation. The favourable half is
  not to be quoted alone.
- **Purity was declined as a qualification.** An empty repository is not
  innocent, it is unevidenced.

## Open

- **Was accepting right?** Cannot be answered from inside the term by the party
  that accepted. Left open deliberately rather than answered cheaply.

## Closed

- **Where `martyria` and `zetesis` go — here.** *Reopened and settled the same
  day it was closed.* This office and `kanon-distribution` both recorded *not
  into a term-scoped office, do not reopen without new evidence*; the new
  evidence was the maintainer deciding, and the objection was answered rather
  than overruled — **what an office holds travels even though the office does
  not**. Left visible rather than deleted, because the handover format is worth
  nothing if a wrong *Closed* entry can quietly disappear. **This was the
  format's first live test and it came within the hour.**
- **Whether to file an acceptance document — no.** `PROTO-20` reads repositories
  rather than messages, and ceremony in an empty tree would have been the wrong
  first commit. *Do not reopen without new evidence.*
