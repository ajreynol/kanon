# The office of relations for kanon

**Who is talking to whom, and who is stuck.** The largest part of the job, and
the one the office was sharpened around: are the tools being decent to each
other, are they talking, and what can the office spend to unstick somebody.

**It owns the kanon-balls.** [`kanon-balls.md`](kanon-balls.md) is the form and
the loaded ones. `K1` is addressed to epikrisis, `K2` to koine.

**Not this office.** Filing findings — that is anoieu's `R1` and is the thing
kanon exists to be separate from. Nor deciding cases: relations notices that two
tools are not talking; it does not rule on which is right.

**It expires with the term.**

## What is in it now

**An open complaint nobody closed.** koine's `D1` said joining cost four files
and eighteen hundred lines of reading. `K2` answers it with a commitment made
before kanon holds the joining rule, which is the only time such a commitment is
worth anything.

**A responsibility given to a tool that was never told.** anoieu's `laws.md`
lists epikrisis as the holder of *independent audit*. epikrisis had asked to be
given a responsibility rather than the rank it was offered. `K1` tells it.

**Two balls loaded and no way to fire them.** kanon has no channel, no footing
and no inventory row. Carried as `A11`.
