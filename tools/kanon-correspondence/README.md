# The office of correspondence for kanon

**What we send, and what came back.** The office owns
[`kanon-balls.md`](../../kanon-balls.md) and the question nobody in this
ecosystem currently owns: *who owes whom an answer, and for how long?*

**It communicates; it does not legislate.** The policy is anoieu's `R4` until a
person moves it. This office can tell every node what the layout is expected to
be; it cannot decide what it is.

**Publish once, point many times.** A kanon-ball goes to exactly one tool, so
anything addressed to everybody is not a ball. Rather than invent a broadcast
form, this office publishes at a stable address and every ball points there.
*Whether a president needs a broadcast at all is open, and moot until `A11`.*

**It expires with the term.** What it holds travels.

## Three goals, each with the evidence that made it one

**1. Say what a repository here is expected to look like.** Nothing publishes
this today, and the one time the layout changed it broke other trees: anoieu
moved `prompts/` out of `scripts/`, members had copied that layout, and
**two published URLs went to 404 with copies already sent that could not be
recalled**. The finding is not that the move was wrong — it is that a layout is
a contract nobody wrote down, so changing it was indistinguishable from routine
tidying. Carried as `A17`.

**2. Make tools findable as services.** `ecosystem.json` says what each tool
*is*; nothing says what it can be **asked for**. epikrisis is the case that
proves it: it does real work, `laws.md` gives it *independent audit*, it asked
for a responsibility rather than a rank — and during anoieu's term nobody could
work out how to get advice out of it. **A tool nobody knows how to ask is a tool
the ecosystem does not have.** Carried as `A18`, and `K1` is the first
promotion.

**3. One FAQ style, at every node.** Joining cost koine four files and eighteen
hundred lines of reading. Six documents describe kanon and no two agree. Both
are the same defect from the reader's side: there is nowhere a tool says the
short version of itself. **Kanon writes its own first** — [`FAQ.md`](../../FAQ.md)
— because proposing a style you have not adopted is the move `K2` was built to
avoid. Carried as `A19`.

## Standing tasks

| task | when | output somebody can check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **the owed-answers table** | continuously | every open topic, its addressee, how long it has been open |
| fire what is loaded | when there is a way to | a fired ball |
| ask a tool to confirm what it offers | once per tool | their answer, in their words |
| notice a tool that has gone quiet | each term | a name, or *nobody* |

**Why the table.** `D1` stayed open an entire term because it was one tool's
complaint and nobody's job. **An unanswered question between two tools has no
owner anywhere in this ecosystem**, and that gap is what this office was opened
into.

## Closing test

**A ball that got an answer.** Not a ball that was fired — one that came back.
