# The office of readiness for kanon

**Is anybody blocked, and are we.** A recurring question with a yes or a no and
the evidence underneath it.

**It already has a counterpart.** anoieu's CI carries a job called
`Ready — init_eo kanon`, which is the same question asked from the other end.
This office is what answers it from this side.

**Not this office.** Fixing what it finds. Readiness reports a colour; the work
is somebody's, and usually not kanon's.

**It expires with the term.**

## What is in it now

**kanon is not ready, and the honest word is `unknown`.** This repository runs
nothing. `PROTO-20` requires CI passing on both sides of a handoff with no
exceptions, and states that a repository running nothing is `unknown` — which
sits with *attention* rather than with *ok*. **No CI is not passing CI.**

That is the whole of the readiness position today, and it is not a complaint
about anybody. It is the one item on [`../../actionable.md`](../../actionable.md)
that kanon can fix alone, `A5`, and it is the reason the stub in anoieu should
stay.

**anoieu is green.** Ten consecutive successful runs on 2026-09-02, after a red
streak of 112. Recorded here because a handoff binds both sides and the party
doing the deleting is bound harder.

## The recurring form

Three lines, whenever it is asked:

- **Them:** who is blocked on what, and whether they know.
- **Us:** what colour this tree reports, and whether anybody looked.
- **Between:** what a handoff would fail on today.
