# The office of the record for kanon

**What happened, kept current while it happens.** `history.md` for the term
kanon holds it, the working summary law 14 makes the president's first
responsibility, and the census law 8 requires.

**It quotes and does not produce.** Law 9 forbids the president from analysing
its own commits; the census belongs to epikrisis and this office asks for it.
**A figure this office cannot get is a figure the ecosystem does not have**, and
saying so is worth more than an estimate the author made about itself.

**It owns what crosses to the next president** — the four-heading `handover.md`
each office keeps, and the closing of the term entry, which is the last act of a
presidency rather than the first act of the next one.

**Not this office.** [`report-card.md`](https://github.com/ajreynol/anoieu/blob/main/docs/report-card.md)
— `laws.md` says in terms that the assessment travels with nobody and stays with
anoieu while the history follows the presidency. Nor a changelog; the commits
are better at that.

**Every figure must be re-derivable by somebody else.** Law 10, and the only
guard a page written by the party it describes actually has.

## Why any president needs it

The record is the one artifact that outlives every term and is read by people
who were not there. A president that keeps it badly costs its successors more
than it costs itself, which is exactly the shape of obligation that needs an
office rather than good intentions.
