# The office of distribution for kanon

**No tool should hold what another tool could.** That sentence is the
ecosystem's stated mission, and this office is the only one whose whole job is
to act on it: find what one tool is carrying that somebody else could carry, and
say so out loud.

**Primary goal: safely offload anoieu.** Fifteen of twenty-eight roles and
twenty-one of twenty-two board items sit in one tree. Every term this office
names at least one thing that could leave it, and *safely* is the load-bearing
word — a handoff onto a tree that cannot answer for it moves a problem rather
than a role.

## The ambition: fast avenues, because latency is the real constraint

**anoieu cannot offload what nobody can answer about quickly.** That is the
finding this office is built around. Willingness has never been the blocker —
the maintainer has been inclined to move governance since 2026-08-31 — and the
thing actually in the way is that a question put to another tool takes hours to
land and can sit a whole term unanswered. **A handoff is a conversation with a
deadline, and this ecosystem does not have a fast conversation.**

So the ambition is not a side project. **Building robust, fast avenues of
communication is the enabling work for every offload this office will ever
propose**, and an office that only ever names roles while the channels stay slow
will keep proposing moves that cannot be completed.

**And this office does not build them.** `koine` exists to be *the shared
machinery of the reporting loop, so the protocol has one implementation rather
than one per member*. It is a member, it is running, and it is the right party.
Kanon building a second implementation would be this office breaking its own
mission on its first task.

**Naming is not taking.** A handoff is seven steps in `roles.md` and a person's
decision at the end of them; what happens here is the first — *name the roles,
not the files* — and nothing after it.

**It points at us too.** An office of distribution that only ever finds load in
somebody else's tree has become a complaints department.

## Standing tasks

| task | when | output somebody can check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| publish the load figure | each term, and on change | the count, with the table it is derived from |
| nominate **one** thing to leave anoieu | each term | a named role or project, and who could hold it |
| measure how long an answer takes | each term | a latency figure from the public record |
| ask koine what fast would take | now — `K3` | koine's answer, or its refusal |
| turn the question on kanon | each term | what kanon holds that another tool could |

## Why any president needs it

**The presidency is itself the first output of this office.** It moved because
one tree held most of the ecosystem's work, and the argument that moved it —
role concentration, the judge sitting inside the judged, a cost compounding each
term — is a distribution argument and nothing else.
